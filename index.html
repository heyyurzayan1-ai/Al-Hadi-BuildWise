Contractor workspace

  1. Create a client project and record verified site measurements.
  2. Generate concept layouts and compare design alternatives.
  3. Send structural and technical items for licensed professional review.
  4. Prepare BOQ, material estimate, labor and project schedule.
  5. Record site photos, expenses, daily progress and variations.
  6. Share client reports and final handover documents.

Initial 3–4 month MVP roadmap

Weeks 1–2: requirements, data model, dimensions, units and scale.

Weeks 3–5: editable 2D plans and PDF/DXF export.

Weeks 6–8: basic 3D walls, doors, windows and camera view.

Weeks 9–10: starter BOQ and dated city-wise Pakistan price data.

Weeks 11–12: AI assistant, photo workflow, safety checklist and contractor dashboard.

Weeks 13–16: testing, feedback, fixes and pilot.

This is an MVP plan, not a promise that a complete professional architecture, structural and mechanical CAD suite can be finished in four months.

Safety rules

  • AI must not approve foundations, columns, beams or load-bearing walls without a qualified structural engineer.
  • Underground wiring and plumbing require site survey and applicable local standards.
  • Show material prices with date, city, quality and supplier source; never invent live rates.
  • Final structural, electrical, fire and building-code approval must come from qualified professionals.
